TikTok integration

Publishing to TikTok

Studio uses two TikTok for Developers products to post the owner's finished videos to his own TikTok profile: Login Kit, to connect his TikTok account, and the Content Posting API with Direct Post, to publish a video he has prepared in Studio.

Studio only posts to the TikTok account its owner connected himself. It never posts on its own initiative and never reads the data of other TikTok users.

Products and scopes

What Studio asks TikTok for

Login Kit for Desktop

Connects the owner's TikTok account with OAuth 2.0 and PKCE. The sign-in page opens in his browser on tiktok.com, and TikTok sends him back to Studio running on his own computer (127.0.0.1).

Content Posting API, Direct Post

Publishes a video file from his computer to his own profile. The file is uploaded with the FILE_UPLOAD method, in one or more chunks, then Studio checks the publishing status until TikTok confirms the post.

user.info.basic

Shows the display name and profile picture of the connected account, so the owner always sees which account he is posting as. Fields used: open_id, display_name, avatar_url.

video.upload

Sends the video file the owner selected for a post to TikTok.

video.publish

Publishes that video directly to the owner's own profile with the caption, cover frame and settings he chose for that post.

Before each post

What the owner chooses himself

When TikTok is ticked for a video, Studio shows a TikTok panel built from TikTok's creator info for the connected account. The video is not sent until the required choices are made.

  • Posting as. The display name, username and avatar of the connected TikTok account, read from TikTok's creator info when the panel opens.
  • Who can view this video. A list of the privacy options TikTok returns for this account. There is no default: the owner has to pick one, and the post is blocked until he does.
  • Interactions. Comments, Duet and Stitch are separate switches, all off by default. An interaction that is turned off in the account's TikTok settings is shown greyed out.
  • Video length. If the video is longer than the maximum TikTok allows for this account, Studio says so and does not send it.
  • Commercial content disclosure. A switch for a video that promotes a brand, product or service, with two options: the owner's own brand, or branded content for a partner. Studio shows the label TikTok will add (Promotional content or Paid partnership). At least one option must be selected, and branded content cannot be set to "Only me".
  • Consent. At the end of the panel Studio shows "By posting, you agree to TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation" (in Polish, like the rest of the app), linked to TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation page, plus TikTok's Branded Content Policy when branded content is selected.
  • Sending. The video is posted only when the owner clicks Publish now, or at the date and time he set for it. Afterwards Studio tells him it may take a few minutes before the post appears on his profile.
Data

Which TikTok data is used, and why

Account identity

Open ID, display name, username and avatar. Used to show which account is connected and to confirm, after sign-in, that the token belongs to that account.

Posting settings

Available privacy options, interaction settings and maximum video length from creator info. Used to build the TikTok panel and to check each post before it is sent.

The video and its settings

The video file, caption, cover frame, privacy, interaction and disclosure choices. Sent to TikTok only to create the post the owner asked for.

Post status

The publish ID and status returned by TikTok. Used to show whether the video was published.

Access tokens

Used to act on the owner's behalf. Saved only locally on his computer, never shown in full in the app.

Studio does not read the owner's videos, followers or messages, and it does not access any data of other TikTok users. Nothing is sold or passed on to third parties. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Disconnect and delete

How to disconnect TikTok and delete the data

  1. In Studio

    Settings, TikTok, the Disconnect button (Rozłącz). It immediately deletes the saved tokens and the account details from the owner's computer.

  2. In the TikTok app

    Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions. Removing Kuba Studio there revokes the app's access on TikTok's side.

  3. Posted videos

    Videos that were already published stay on TikTok as normal posts and can be edited or deleted in the TikTok app like any other video.
