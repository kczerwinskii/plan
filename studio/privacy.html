Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy of the Studio app

Studio is a private desktop application of Jakub Czerwiński (online personal trainer, Instagram @kubaczerwinskii), used exclusively by its owner to publish his own videos to his own accounts on Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and TikTok and to view the statistics of those accounts. The application has no other users.

YouTube API Services

Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using the application, the owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

What YouTube data is used: after signing in with Google (OAuth 2.0), the application receives an access token to the owner's YouTube account and uses it to upload his own videos to his own channel and to read the public statistics of that channel (number of subscribers, views and videos). The application does not read data of other YouTube users or data about viewers.

Where it is stored: tokens and statistics are saved only locally, on the owner's computer, in the application folder. They are not sent to any server other than the servers of Google and of the other services to which the owner publishes content. Statistical data retrieved from YouTube is refreshed each time the view is opened and is stored for no longer than 30 days; the owner's own daily records of the subscriber count are the owner's aggregated data.

Revoking access: the owner can disconnect YouTube at any time with the "Rozłącz" (Disconnect) button in the application settings (this deletes the tokens) or revoke consent at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

TikTok (Login Kit, Content Posting API)

What TikTok data is used: after signing in with TikTok (OAuth 2.0), the application receives an access token to the owner's TikTok account (scopes user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish). It uses it only to display the name and profile picture of the connected account, to retrieve the posting settings of that account (available privacy levels, limits) and to publish his own videos to his own profile with the caption, privacy and interaction settings chosen by the owner before each post. The application does not read videos, followers, messages or data of other TikTok users.

Storage and deletion: TikTok tokens are stored only locally on the owner's computer. The "Rozłącz" (Disconnect) button in the application settings deletes them immediately; access can also be revoked in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions).

In short: Studio is a private desktop app used only by its owner, Jakub Czerwinski, to publish his own videos to his own TikTok account. With the owner's consent via TikTok Login Kit it uses the scopes user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish only to show the connected account's name and avatar, read the account's posting settings, and post the owner's videos with the caption, privacy level and interaction settings he selects before each post. No data of other TikTok users is accessed, stored, sold or shared. Tokens are stored only locally on the owner's computer and are deleted with the "Rozłącz" (Disconnect) button; access can also be revoked in the TikTok app settings.

Other services

For Instagram, Facebook and TikTok the application processes data in the same way: access tokens to the owner's own accounts, public statistics of his own posts and the video files he sends himself. It does not collect data of other people and does not pass anything on to third parties.

Cookies and tracking

The application does not use cookies or tracking tools. The website on which you are reading this policy is static and does not collect data.
