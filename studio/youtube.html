YouTube integration

Publishing to YouTube

Studio uses YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API v3) to upload the owner's finished videos to his own YouTube channel and to show the basic statistics of that channel.

Works with YouTube. See the Privacy Policy, the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
Scopes

What Studio asks Google for

youtube.upload

Uploads a video the owner selected to his own channel, with the title, description and privacy setting he chose for it.

youtube.readonly

Reads the name and public statistics of his own channel: subscribers, views and number of videos.

Before each upload the owner sets the YouTube title (up to 100 characters) and the privacy: private, which is the default, unlisted or public. The upload starts only when he clicks Publish now, or at the date and time he set.

Data and access

Where the data stays and how to revoke access

  • Tokens and statistics are stored only locally, on the owner's computer. Statistics are refreshed each time the view is opened and kept for no longer than 30 days.
  • Studio does not read data of other YouTube users or data about viewers.
  • To disconnect: Settings, YouTube, the Disconnect button (Rozłącz), which deletes the tokens. Access can also be revoked at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

Full details are in the Privacy Policy.
